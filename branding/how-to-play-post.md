## Body (copy everything below this line)

Someone farted in the lift. Six people. One guess. Can you find the farter?

**A new case appears every day at 00:00 UTC.** Look for the pinned "Who Farted? Case #…" post.

### How to play

1. **Tap a person** to read their clue. The clue lights up the lift, so you can see what it means.
2. **Mark the innocent.** If the clues prove someone did NOT do it, tap ✗ MARK INNOCENT. This is just your notes, not your guess. (If the clues don't prove it yet, they'll say "Prove it!")
3. **Guess the farter.** You get **ONE** guess. Make it count!

New here? The game shows you the six suspects, then a 1-minute practice case.

### The lift

No left or right! Places are named after things in the lift: the **BUTTONS** panel, the **MIDDLE**, the **MIRROR**, the **BACK** wall and the **DOORS**. Food clues use the lunch stickers: CHILI, BEANS, EGGS…

Every clue also shows **in plain words**, with a **"So:"** line that tells you what it means.

### Days of the week

- **Monday & Tuesday:** easy, 4 suspects
- **Wednesday to Friday:** medium, 6 suspects
- **Saturday & Sunday:** hard. **The farter LIES.** Everyone else tells the truth.

### Streaks and badges

Guess right on days in a row to build a streak. Your badge shows next to your name here:

Sniffer → Rookie Sniffer → Detective → Senior Sniffer → Chief Inspector → **Legendary Nose**

### After you play

- See **who the whole sub blamed** (spoiler: usually Agnes).
- Tap **POST MY VERDICT** to share your time and streak in the comments. It never reveals the answer.
- Got it wrong? The game shows you **how to solve it**, step by step, and why your pick was innocent.
