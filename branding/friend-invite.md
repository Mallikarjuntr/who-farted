# Inviting friends to the private test subreddit

## Steps (you, on reddit.com)

1. Ask each friend for their **Reddit username** (they need a Reddit account).
2. Open https://www.reddit.com/r/who_farted_daily_dev/ → **Mod Tools** → **Approved users** (sometimes called "Approved members").
3. Tap **Add user**, type their username, and save. Repeat for each friend.
4. Send them the message below.

They can now see and play the private test subreddit. They will **not** see the TEST MODE bar; only moderators see it.

## Message to send (copy and paste)

Hey! I'm building a little Reddit game for a hackathon and I'd love your honest opinion. 🙏

It's called **Who Farted?** Six people in a lift, one of them farted, and you read the clues to find out who. One guess a day.

👉 https://www.reddit.com/r/who_farted_daily_dev/

Tap the newest pinned post and press **START SNIFFING**. It takes 2–4 minutes.

Please play for a few days (there's a new case every day), and tell me:
1. Was anything confusing in the first minute?
2. Was it too easy or too hard?
3. Was it funny?
4. Did anything look broken (phone or computer)?

Brutal honesty welcome. Thanks! 😄
